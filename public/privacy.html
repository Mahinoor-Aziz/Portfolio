Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Contact form

If you send a message, your name, email address and message are stored privately on this site's server so I can reply. They are not shared or sold. To have your message deleted, ask me through the contact form.

AI Recruiter chat

Questions you type in the chat are sent to OpenAI to generate an answer from my portfolio facts. This site does not store the conversation. Please do not type sensitive personal information into the chat.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers. To limit abuse, your IP address is held briefly in server memory to apply rate limits.

Third-party resources

Fonts (Google Fonts) and a 3D library (cdnjs) load from their servers, which means those providers can see your IP address when the page loads.
